# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 7, 2026

## 1. Who We Are

QuizOps ("we", "us", "our") is operated by AutoShiftOps (Sudhakar Sajja), based in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada. QuizOps is an AI-powered quiz platform accessible at https://quiz.autoshiftops.com.

Contact: admin@autoshiftops.com

## 2. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use QuizOps as a reader or publisher.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide
- **Account information:** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google OAuth.
- **Quiz content:** Article URLs and text you submit for quiz generation.
- **Quiz responses:** Answers you submit when taking a quiz, scores, and time taken.
- **Publisher profile:** Username, display name, bio, and website URL you provide when setting up a publisher account.

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage data:** Pages visited, quiz attempts, generation requests, timestamps.
- **Device information:** Browser type, operating system, IP address.
- **Cookies:** Session cookies for authentication. Analytics cookies for Google Analytics (see Section 6).

### 3.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Payment information (not processed by QuizOps directly).
- Sensitive personal information (health, financial, government ID).

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and operate the QuizOps platform
- Save and display your quiz progress and scores
- Generate quizzes from article content using AI (see Section 5)
- Send you email notifications about your account (when applicable)
- Improve the platform through usage analytics
- Enforce our Terms of Service and content moderation policies

## 5. AI Processing — Important Disclosure

**When you submit an article URL or text for quiz generation:**

- The article text is sent to **OpenAI's API** (GPT-4o mini) for question generation.
- OpenAI processes this text on their servers according to their Privacy Policy (openai.com/policies/privacy-policy).
- We do not store your article text after the quiz is generated — it is used only for the generation request.
- By using the quiz generation feature, you confirm you have the right to submit the article content for processing.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

- All user data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS in the US region).
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
- We implement Row-Level Security (RLS) — users can only access their own data.
- We do not sell your data to any third party.

## 8. Data Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
- Quiz attempt data is retained indefinitely to provide score history.
- You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 10).

## 9. Cookies

We use:
- **Essential cookies:** Required for authentication and session management. Cannot be disabled.
- **Analytics cookies:** Google Analytics to understand usage patterns. You may opt out via your browser settings or Google's opt-out tool.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and associated data.
- **Portability:** Request your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection:** Object to processing of your data for analytics purposes.

**To exercise any of these rights, email: admin@autoshiftops.com**

We will respond within 30 days.

## 11. GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR. Our legal basis for processing your data is:
- **Contract:** Processing necessary to provide the QuizOps service.
- **Legitimate interests:** Analytics to improve the service.
- **Consent:** Analytics cookies (you may withdraw consent at any time).

## 12. Canadian Privacy Law (PIPEDA)

As a Canadian-based service, we comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). You may contact our Privacy Officer at admin@autoshiftops.com for any PIPEDA-related requests.

## 13. Children's Privacy

QuizOps is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, by email.
